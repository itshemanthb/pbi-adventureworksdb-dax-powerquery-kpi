
# 📊 AdventureWorks Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

### 1. Project Title / Headline

📈 **AdventureWorks Sales Analytics: Interactive Business Intelligence Dashboard**

A dynamic, interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, revenue, profitability, customer behavior, and product trends using AdventureWorks sales data. The report provides executive-level insights through KPI tracking, sales target comparisons, customer segmentation, product analysis, and geographic visualizations.

### 2. Short Description / Purpose

The AdventureWorks Sales Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report developed to help users monitor business performance and explore key sales metrics across products, customers, and geographic regions. The dashboard focuses on tracking total revenue, profit, orders, returns, customer trends, and sales targets, enabling users to identify performance patterns and make data-driven business decisions.

The project includes dedicated pages for executive performance monitoring, product-level analysis, geographic sales distribution, and customer insights. It is designed for business analysts, sales managers, and decision-makers who want to understand sales performance and identify opportunities for business growth.

### 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

- 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Main data visualization platform used to build the interactive report and dashboards.
- 📂 **Power Query** – Data preparation and transformation layer used to clean and organize data for analysis.
- 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Used to create measures for total revenue, total profit, total orders, total returns, customer metrics, target comparisons, and other analytical calculations.
- 🗂️ **Data Modeling** – Relationships among product, customer, calendar, territory, and sales-related tables support filtering and cross-analysis.
- 📈 **Interactive Visualizations** – KPI cards, line charts, area charts, bar charts, donut charts, tables, gauges, maps, and slicers used to explore business performance.
- 🎛️ **Power BI Interactivity** – Filters, slicers, page navigation, and drill-through functionality support detailed exploration of the report.
- 📁 **File Formats** – `.pbix` for the Power BI project and `.png` for dashboard screenshots and previews.

### 4. Data Source

**Source: AdventureWorks Sales Dataset**

The dashboard uses AdventureWorks-style business data organized around sales transactions, products, customers, dates, and geographic territories.

The data covers the following analytical areas:

- **Sales Performance** – Revenue, profit, orders, and returns.
- **Product Information** – Product names, categories, and subcategories.
- **Customer Information** – Customer details, income levels, occupations, and customer-level sales performance.
- **Geographic Information** – Countries and territories used to analyze the distribution of orders.
- **Calendar Information** – Date, month, week, and year fields used for time-based analysis.
- **Business Targets** – Revenue, profit, and order targets used to compare actual performance against goals.

The dashboard combines these data elements to provide a unified view of business performance and support interactive analysis across multiple dimensions.

### 5. Features / Highlights

**• Business Problem**

Businesses generate large amounts of sales data, but understanding overall performance, identifying profitable products, monitoring customer behavior, and tracking targets can be difficult when information is spread across multiple tables and reports.

Important business questions include:

- How are revenue, profit, and order volumes performing over time?
- Are monthly sales and order targets being achieved?
- Which product categories and individual products contribute the most orders and revenue?
- How does sales performance vary across geographic regions?
- Which customer segments generate the most orders and revenue?
- How are customer trends and product returns changing over time?

The dashboard addresses these questions through interactive visualizations and consolidated performance indicators.

**• Goal of the Dashboard**

To build an interactive business intelligence solution that:

- Provides a centralized view of key sales and profitability KPIs.
- Tracks revenue, profit, orders, and returns over time.
- Compares actual performance against business targets.
- Identifies important products, categories, and subcategories.
- Analyzes customer purchasing patterns and segmentation.
- Visualizes geographic order distribution.
- Enables users to filter and explore business performance for more informed decision-making.

**• Walkthrough of Key Visuals**

- **Executive Dashboard (Overview)**

  Provides a high-level summary of business performance using KPI cards for total orders, total profit, and total revenue. The page also includes revenue trends, monthly revenue and order indicators, return metrics, and product category analysis.

- **Revenue Trending (Line Chart)**

  Displays revenue performance over time, helping users identify sales growth patterns, fluctuations, and periods of stronger or weaker performance.

- **Monthly Revenue and Orders (KPI Visuals)**

  Compares current performance with previous-month measures for revenue and orders, supporting month-over-month performance monitoring.

- **Orders by Category (Bar Chart)**

  Compares order volumes across product categories, helping users understand which categories generate greater demand.

- **Top 10 Products (Table)**

  Highlights leading products using order and revenue measures, allowing users to compare product-level performance.

- **Product Details Page**

  Provides a dedicated view for exploring individual products and their associated metrics, including subcategory information and product-related performance analysis.

- **Monthly Orders vs Targets (Gauge)**

  Compares total orders against the defined order target and provides an at-a-glance view of progress toward the goal.

- **Monthly Revenue vs Targets (Gauge)**

  Tracks revenue against the revenue target to help users assess sales performance relative to expectations.

- **Monthly Profit vs Targets (Gauge)**

  Compares actual profit with the profit target, supporting profitability monitoring and performance evaluation.

- **Profit Trending (Line Chart)**

  Visualizes profit over time and includes an adjusted profit measure for examining profitability patterns.

- **Product Returns Analysis (Area Chart)**

  Shows return-related trends across time periods and supports analysis using the available product metric selection.

- **Geographic Sales Analysis (Map)**

  Displays order distribution by country, helping users explore geographic differences and identify regions contributing to order volumes.

- **Customer Details Page – KPI Cards**

  Includes unique customer counts and average revenue per customer, providing an overview of customer activity and value.

- **Customer Trends (Line Chart)**

  Tracks customer counts over time and supports analysis of customer-related metrics using interactive selections.

- **Orders by Income Level (Donut Chart)**

  Segments orders by customer income level to help users understand purchasing activity across different income groups.

- **Orders by Occupation (Donut Chart)**

  Breaks down orders by customer occupation, providing insight into the purchasing patterns of different customer segments.

- **Top 100 Customers (Table)**

  Presents customer-level information alongside order and revenue measures, helping users identify and compare valuable customers.

- **Interactive Filters and Navigation**

  Report slicers, page navigation buttons, and selection controls enable users to move between the executive dashboard, product details, customer details, and geographic analysis.

**• Business Impact & Insights**

- **Sales Performance Monitoring:** Track revenue, profit, orders, and returns from a centralized dashboard.
- **Target Achievement:** Compare actual revenue, profit, and order volumes against business targets to identify performance gaps.
- **Product Optimization:** Identify leading products and categories to support inventory planning, product strategy, and sales optimization.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Analyze customer purchasing patterns by income level and occupation to support targeted marketing strategies.
- **Customer Value Analysis:** Use revenue-per-customer and customer-level performance measures to understand customer contribution.
- **Geographic Analysis:** Explore order distribution across countries to identify strong markets and potential areas for expansion.
- **Trend Analysis:** Monitor changes in sales, profitability, and customer metrics over time to support business planning.
- **Data-Driven Decision-Making:** Replace fragmented reporting with interactive visual analysis that helps stakeholders investigate performance and make informed decisions.
